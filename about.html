Hi, I’m Foster

I started Brackish to help small teams get the tools big organizations take for granted.

By day I lead property management for a multi-office operation along the Georgia coast. That work taught me where small teams really lose time: reports pulled by hand, information nobody can find, and websites that don’t pull their weight.

I’ve spent years building websites, dashboards and AI-powered tools to fix exactly those problems, first for my own teams and for the nonprofits I serve. I sit on the board of SOAR Golden Isles, where I chair public relations and look after the organization’s website and digital tools.

Brackish brings that same work to small businesses and nonprofits across the Golden Isles and beyond: practical, affordable, and explained in plain English.
