Nonprofit · Website + custom portal

SOAR Golden Isles

Website redesignCustom web appSecure logins

SOAR Golden Isles serves individuals with developmental disabilities. Its site ran on Wix, and the board needed a better first impression plus one place to share information with board members and volunteers.

What we built

  • A redesigned homepage with photo storytelling, scroll animations and animated impact numbers, built to work inside their existing Wix site.
  • A password-protected Board & Volunteer Portal, built from scratch, with separate access for board members, volunteers and admins.
  • Content the team can update themselves, stored in a secure database instead of scattered emails and documents.
